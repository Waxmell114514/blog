My first project as an Undergraduate Research Assistant at PeiLab. Reward models
are the component that turns human preference data into a signal a policy can
actually be optimised against, which makes them a small but load-bearing part of
the post-training stack.

I trained reward models and ran the surrounding model-training experiments in
PyTorch, working through the practical side of the loop: preparing preference
data, training and tuning the models, and evaluating how well the learned reward
tracked the preferences it was fit to.
