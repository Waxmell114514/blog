Research moves at the speed of its infrastructure. I built and maintained
end-to-end training pipelines for **vision-language-action (VLA)** and
**LLM-based** models — the path from raw data through training runs to
evaluation — so that experiments could be launched, reproduced, and compared
without rebuilding the plumbing each time.
