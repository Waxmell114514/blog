I have been keeping research notes in scratch files for a while now, and they are
not much use to anyone there — including me, six months later. So this is the
public version.

What I expect to write about
======
Mostly **mechanistic interpretability**: sparse autoencoders, activation patching,
circuit discovery, and what actually happens when you try these methods on a model
rather than reading about them. I expect a fair number of posts to be
"here is the thing I misunderstood and what fixed it".

Some of it will be about the training side too — reward models, distillation,
and the unglamorous business of keeping training pipelines working — since that
is what I spend my days on in the lab.

Why in public
======
Writing something down for a reader forces a level of precision that a private
note never does. If a post is wrong, I would rather find out.
